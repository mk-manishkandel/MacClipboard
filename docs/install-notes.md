## Install

1. Download **MacClipboard.zip** below and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **MacClipboard.app** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. Because the app isn't notarized by Apple, macOS blocks it the first time:
   - **macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:** click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
     **Open Anyway** next to "MacClipboard was blocked", then confirm with your password.
   - **macOS 14 Sonoma:** right-click the app, choose **Open**, then click **Open**.
4. When asked, allow **Accessibility** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility** so it can paste for you.
5. Press **⇧⌘C** to open your clipboard history.

Already installed? The app updates itself: open **Settings → General → Check now**.
